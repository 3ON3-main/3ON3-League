Rulebook

What Is This "New Style"?

Based on the new 3 on 3, 3ICE format. Designed to simulate fast-paced, overtime hockey throughout the entire game with almost zero whistles and maximum speed!

01

Overtime All The Time (3-on-3 Full Time)

3 skaters plus a goalie for each team for the entire duration. No standard 5-on-5 lulls—pure open-ice skating and scoring opportunities.

02

No Powerplays – Jailbreak Penalty Shots

Instead of traditional powerplays, infractions result in a jailbreak penalty shot. Defending players start from the blue line while the penalty shot taker starts from the center dot. As soon as the puck is touched, it’s a free game!

03

Shootouts Instead of Overtime

If regulation ends in a tie, we go straight to one-round shootouts until a winner is determined.

04

Goalies & Puck Play (Anything Can Happen)

Goalies can play the puck anywhere on the ice. If the puck hits the netting or boards and stays on the ice surface, play continues uninterrupted.

05

Intentional Icing & Empty Net Misses

Intentional icing is a penalty resulting in a penalty shot (honest hockey play attempts that miss are fine). Additionally, shooting on an empty net from behind center ice and missing also results in a penalty shot.

06

Offensive Zone Exit = Turnover

If the offensive team brings the puck back out of the offensive zone across the blue line without defensive interference, it is ruled a turnover. The whistle blows and the offending player must stop touching the puck until the opponent touches it first.

07

Goalie Resets & Clear Saves

Once a goalie makes a clear save, play pauses for a goalie reset. Opposing players must retreat above the face-off circle—no hovering, hacking, or sneaking up behind the goalie during restarts.

08

Clearing the Zone After Scoring

After a goal is scored, the scoring team must clear the zone before they can re-enter and attack again.
